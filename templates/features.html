What's inside StudyHub

Everything you need to study with the right people, stay organized, and see yourself grow.

StudyHub brings together study-partner matching, a shared homework tracker, a community feed, and progress tracking — in one place, built for students.

Find your people

Not random matchmaking — explainable compatibility. StudyHub matches you with classmates whose strong subjects are your weak ones, whose schedule overlaps yours, and who learn the way you do — and tells you exactly why.

Study buddy matching

Matched on subjects, schedule, and learning style — with the reasoning shown, not hidden.

Connections

Build a real network of classmates you study and share progress with.

Study threads

Join subject-based group chats with people working on the same material.

Get organized, together

A personal assignment tracker with a priority list that re-sorts itself, a peer-help marketplace for when you're stuck, and live study sessions where "studying together" actually means together.

Homework tracker

Every assignment in one list, automatically prioritized by what's due and what's urgent.

Peer help marketplace

Share an assignment for help, or offer help on someone else's — and build reputation doing it.

Live study sessions

One goal, one timer, one shared notepad — visible to everyone in the session in real time.

Watch yourself grow

Reputation, badges, and a weekly leaderboard turn effort you were already putting in into something you can actually see add up.

Reputation & levels

Earn reputation for helping others, answering questions, and staying active.

Badges

Unlock badges for streaks, milestones, and being a reliable member of the community.

Leaderboard

See how you stack up against your department, your connections, and the wider community.
